The primary differences between the old CSV schema and the new UOM (Unit of Measure) CSV schema center on **dual-barcode tracking**, **formal ERP unit accounting**, and **column positioning**. --- ### Column Count:** Increased from 21 columns to 22 columns. * **Barcode Expansion:** Instead of a single barcode column, the new CSV has separate columns for `Base UPC` (single item) and `Bulk UPC` (master carton). * **Column Order Shift:** The UOM definitions (`baseUom`, `bulkUom`, `uomConversion`) were moved up to columns 5, 6, and 7 (immediately following Brand Name), whereas the old version placed unit definitions after inventory counts. * **Scan Multiplier Logic:** Scanning a `Bulk UPC` from a CSV-imported item automatically multiplies incoming/outgoing quantities by the `UOM Conversion Factor` when adjusting `Book Inventory` and `Physical Count`.
